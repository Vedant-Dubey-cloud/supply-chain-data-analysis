# Supply Chain Data Analysis

## Overview

This project performs an exploratory data analysis (EDA) of a supply chain dataset to uncover insights related to product performance, supplier efficiency, transportation costs, and profitability. Using Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques, the project identifies key business trends and operational improvement opportunities within the supply chain.

The analysis focuses on understanding how different factors influence revenue generation, manufacturing costs, lead times, defect rates, and overall supply chain performance.

---

## Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Explore and understand the structure of the supply chain dataset.
- Identify patterns and relationships among operational variables.
- Evaluate supplier performance based on lead times and defect rates.
- Analyze transportation methods in terms of cost and efficiency.
- Determine the most profitable products and product categories.
- Generate actionable insights to support business decision-making.

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains information related to:

- Product categories and SKUs
- Revenue generated
- Manufacturing costs
- Number of products sold
- Supplier details
- Lead times
- Defect rates
- Transportation modes and shipping costs
- Customer demographics

Dataset Source: Kaggle Supply Chain Dataset

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Collection

The dataset is downloaded directly from Kaggle using KaggleHub and loaded into a Pandas DataFrame for analysis.

### 2. Data Exploration

Initial exploration includes:

- Dataset dimensions
- Column information
- Data types
- Missing value inspection
- Descriptive statistics

### 3. Correlation Analysis

A correlation matrix and heatmap are generated to identify relationships between numerical variables and understand how operational metrics interact.

### 4. Distribution Analysis

Histograms are used to visualize the distribution of numerical features and identify patterns, skewness, and potential anomalies.

### 5. Outlier Detection

The Interquartile Range (IQR) method is applied to detect potential outliers across numerical columns.

### 6. Business Performance Analysis

The project evaluates:

#### Product Performance
- Revenue generated by product category
- Revenue per unit sold
- Profitability analysis

#### Customer Analysis
- Revenue contribution by customer demographics

#### Supplier Analysis
- Average defect rate by supplier
- Average lead time by supplier

#### Transportation Analysis
- Average shipping costs by transportation mode
- Average shipping times by transportation mode

#### Profitability Analysis
- Revenue-to-cost ratio
- Profit generated by product category
- Most profitable SKUs
