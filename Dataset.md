## Dataset

The dataset was downloaded programmatically from the following source:

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/amirmotefaker/supply-chain-dataset?utm_source=chatgpt.com&select=supply_chain_data.csv

The dataset contains supply chain operational metrics including product information, supplier performance, transportation details, manufacturing costs, sales volume, and revenue data.

The data was imported into Python using Pandas and used for exploratory data analysis and business insight generation.
